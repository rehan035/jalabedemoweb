## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the regal heritage and culinary mastery of Lahore’s most celebrated confection. It blends high-end editorial sophistication with the warmth and visceral indulgence of traditional slow-crafted sweets. The visual identity positions the craft as artisanal haute gastronomy—elevating the classic golden spirals into an elite culinary legacy.

The target audience encompasses discerning food connoisseurs, luxury gift buyers, the global Pakistani diaspora seeking authentic culinary nostalgia, and local patrons celebrating life’s milestone moments. Every touchpoint must evoke celebration, reverence for time-tested craftsmanship, warmth, and quiet luxury.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Minimalism** with **Classical South Asian Opulence**:
- Clean, generous ivory and almond canvas layouts, reminiscent of a luxury food quarterly or high-fashion monograph.
- Restrained, razor-sharp hairline borders echoing traditional filigree and polished brass confectionary trays.
- Rich, velvety contrast between deep saffron-infused burgundy backgrounds and warm, glowing honey ambers.
- Tactile, deliberate typography that communicates historical gravitas without feeling antiquated.
- Completely avoids synthetic neon lighting, cold generic tech blues, and muddy dark modes.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the alchemy of traditional confection-making: the deep warmth of cast iron cauldrons, the rich hues of slow-simmering saffron syrups, and the warm ivory tone of fresh clotted cream and unbleached parchment.

### Color Hierarchy & Roles
- **Primary (`#4A151B` - Deep Warm Burgundy):** The core brand anchor. Used for high-impact editorial moments, hero banners, signature buttons, and prominent editorial dividers. It suggests nobility, depth, and generational authority.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Saffron Amber):** The living glow of hot syrup and crisp fried spirals. Reserved for key transactional triggers (like the primary WhatsApp checkout action), status highlights, ratings, and delicate filigree accents.
- **Tertiary (`#2B1D19` - Dark Espresso Brown):** Replaces clinical carbon black. Used for long-form narrative body text, primary metadata, and deep solid containers where burgundy would overwhelm.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7` - Warm Ivory):** The atmospheric canvas of the UI. Lends editorial breathability, warmth, and vintage paper tactile qualities that pure cold white cannot provide.

### Specialized Surface Tokens
- **Background Tiers:** Canvas operates on `#FDFBF7`, cards on `#F8F4EC`, and sub-panels or elevated drawers on `#EFE8DC`.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Never use gray. Hairline borders are composed of `#C27803` at 20%–35% opacity, or `#EFE8DC` over primary surfaces.
- **Text Contrast:** Dominant headlines use `#1C1412` or `#2E0B0E` over cream backgrounds, and pure `#FDFBF7` when reversed against burgundy.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between authoritative historical gravitas and clean modern commerce. 

- **Playfair Display (Display & Headlines):** Conveys the grandeur of royal Mughal kitchens and Lahore's heritage. High contrast letterforms and refined terminal balls mimic classic calligraphic craftsmanship. Use title casing for major titles. Quotes and testimonials must lean into italicized weights to emulate literary publications.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body & Labels):** Provides pristine modern legibility across high-density menus, nutritional details, and mobile transactional cart drawers. It softens the traditional weight of the serif headlines, making the interface approachable and crisp.
- **Badge & Microcopy Rules:** All badges (e.g., "PURE DESI GHEE", "SINCE 1978", "PIPING HOT") must be set in uppercase using `label-badge` with tracking expanded to `0.12em` for an authentic luxury seal finish.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **Disciplined Magazine Grid** characterized by intentional, asymmetric balance and expansive breathing room. 

### Grid Geometry
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Content conforms to a max-width container of `1280px` centered within outer margins (`margin-desktop: 4rem`). Elements span 12 (editorial covers), 8/4 (product showcase and interactive cart summary), or 4/4/4 (collection tiers).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standardized on fluid margins (`margin-tablet: 2rem`) with `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid full-width architecture with `1rem` safe canvas edge margins. Key interactions (such as the branch selector tabs) transition into a horizontally swipeable rail with momentum snapping.

### Rhythmic Cadence
- Space between distinct thematic sections utilizes `space-2xl` to ensure the atmosphere feels unhurried, reflecting the patience of artisanal slow frying.
- Product card interiors leverage tight, crisp spacing (`space-sm` and `space-md`), creating a stark contrast against wide, airy page backgrounds.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold drop shadows and blurry modern software glows. Depth is achieved via **Tonal Layering and Warm Radiant Halos**:

1. **Surface Stratification:**
   - Base canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Natural vellum feel).
   - Elevated content card: `#F8F4EC` bounded by a 1px solid hairline border in `#D97706` at 22% opacity.
   - High-priority interactive elements (Drawer/Modal): `#FFFFFF` or `#2E0B0E` (for dark celebratory panels) lifted by an ambient shadow.

2. **Ambient Saffron Glow:**
   - Shadows are subtly tinted with deep warm mahogany rather than neutral gray. 
   - Standard card elevation: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(46, 11, 14, 0.05), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(46, 11, 14, 0.03)`.
   - Hovered/Interactive state: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(74, 21, 27, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.

3. **Hairline Gold Outlines:**
   - All interactive panels, inputs, and selected branch tabs utilize 1px solid borders in antique amber or honey gold to evoke gilded manuscript borders.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft, Refined Architectural Language** (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Standard interactive components (buttons, input fields, and category chips) use crisp `0.25rem` (4px) corner radii. This maintains an editorial, printed catalog precision, strictly avoiding overly playful bubble corners.
- Large containers, image frames, and the WhatsApp cart slide-out drawer use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px).
- Status badges and product tags can use full oval pill geometry to simulate stamped brass or wax confectionary seals, but structural framing remains square and balanced.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Traditional Order):** Solid Deep Warm Burgundy (`#4A151B`) background, `#FDFBF7` typography, 1px perimeter border in `#C27803` at 40% opacity. Smooth hover transition to `#2E0B0E` with a subtle saffron amber glow.
- **WhatsApp Direct Order CTA:** Vibrant honey-amber to saffron gradient (`#F59E0B` to `#D97706`), deep espresso typography (`#1C1412`), paired with a crisp vector WhatsApp monogram. Bold, warm, and appetizing.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent base, 1px border in `#D97706` (40% opacity), typography in `#4A151B`. On hover, fills with `#EFE8DC` at 50% opacity.

### Product Display Cards
- High-contrast visual cards set on `#F8F4EC` with a delicate `1px solid rgba(194, 120, 3, 0.2)` boundary.
- Visual frame incorporates an aspect-ratio-locked imagery container displaying high-detail photography of glossy, syrup-soaked spirals against marble or brass backgrounds.
- Header contains weight options (e.g., "0.5 KG", "1 KG", "Special Royal Tin") presented as segmented micro-toggles.
- Footer features an inline quick-add action triggering the slide-out WhatsApp cart drawer.

### Interactive WhatsApp Cart Drawer
- Floats from the right viewport edge on desktop, slides up from base on mobile.
- Background: `#FDFBF7` with deep saffron-mahogany top bar (`#2E0B0E`) featuring gold serif typography: *"Your Imperial Confection Box"*.
- Live calculation for delivery slots, freshly fried guarantees, and one-tap generation of formatted WhatsApp order string for instant kitchen routing.

### Verified Lahore Branch Selector
- Tab bar formatted as an archival heritage index (e.g., "Mall Road", "Gulberg", "DHA").
- Unselected tabs: `#EFE8DC` background with `#2B1D19` muted typography.
- Active tab: `#4A151B` background, pure ivory text, with a 2px saffron bottom indicator line and a tiny "Live Frying Now" pulse indicator.

### Editorial Customer Love & Testimonials
- Centered quotes framed with high-contrast serif quotation marks in `#D97706` at 30% opacity.
- Body in `quote-editorial` (`Playfair Display`, italic).
- Verified patron attribution formatted with `label-badge` uppercase lettering, complete with location and city stamps.

### Input Fields & Controls
- Form controls feature `#FDFBF7` background, 1px `#EFE8DC` border, and `#1C1412` typed text.
- Focus states shift the border cleanly to `#D97706` with no aggressive outline, only an ambient `0 0 0 2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2)` soft saffron ring.
